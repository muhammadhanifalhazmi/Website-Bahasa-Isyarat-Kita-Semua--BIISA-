BISINDO


Bahasa Isyarat Indonesia


"The acronym for Bisindo is Bahasa Isyarat Indonesia, which is a natural form of communication within the Deaf community. Bisindo emerges organically within the Deaf community and later develops through observation and research. Due to its natural emergence, Bisindo exhibits variations in gestures across different regions, reflecting the Deaf culture in those areas. Bisindo is conveyed through hand movements, facial expressions, and body language.







Why Learn BISINDO ?











Let's communicate without barriers! In Indonesia, there are more than 2,500,000 deaf individuals, and Indonesian Sign Language (BISINDO) is the most effective form of communication, not limited to the deaf but for everyone. Besides reducing communication barriers and supporting an inclusive environment, learning BISINDO also brings many benefits. What are they? Let's explore them below!
